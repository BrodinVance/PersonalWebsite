## Derivation Goes Here:

Classical:

$A_i(a_n, \lambda)$, $B_i(b_n, \lambda)$

$E(a_n,b_n) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} A_iB_i$

$S = E(a_1,b_1) - E(a_1,b_2) + E(a_2, b_1) + E(a_2,b_2)$

$s = AB - AB' + A'B + A'B'$

$s = A(B - B') + A'(B + B')$

Looking at $B$ and $B'$:

If  $B = B'$, then $B - B' = 0$ and $B + B' = \pm2$

If $B \neq B'$  then $B - B' = \pm2$ and $B + B' = 0$

In both scenarios, $|s| = 2$

$S = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} A_iB_i - \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} A_iB'_i + \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} A'_iB_i +\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} A'_iB'_i$ 

$S = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (AB - AB' + A'B + A'B)$

$S = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} s$

Since  $|s| = 2$,  $|S| = 2$

Quantum:

$E(a_n,b_n) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} A_iB_i$

$E(a_n,b_n) = \lim_{N \to \infty} \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} A_iB_i$

$E(a_n,b_n) = \sum_{A,B \in \pm 1} A_iB_i * P(A,B| a) $
